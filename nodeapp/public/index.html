How it works

1

Report

The tenant describes the problem in their flat and submits it. It gets a reference number straight away.

2

Assign

The manager sets the priority and sends the job to a technician or a vendor company, with a date it should be done by.

3

Fix and close

They update progress, attach photos and record what was done. The manager approves the cost and closes it.

Who it is for

Tenants

Report a problem in your flat and follow its progress. You only ever see your own.

Property managers

See every open request across your properties, assign the work, and keep track of what it costs.

Technicians

See the jobs given to you, update progress, and record what you did and what it cost.

Vendors

Receive work orders for your company, send quotations, and submit invoices for approval.

Ready to start?

Set up your organization, add a property and its units, invite your tenants and technicians, and run a repair from report to closed.
